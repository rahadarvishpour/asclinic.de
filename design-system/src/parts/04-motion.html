The rule

Nothing on a Glowvie page appears — everything arrives. Content fades up as it enters the viewport, siblings arrive in sequence rather than together, decorative shapes never fully settle, and every interactive surface answers within 320ms. Motion is where the brand's warmth lives; it is also the first thing to switch off for anyone who asks, which tokens.css does globally through prefers-reduced-motion.

7 · Parallax — scroll inside the frame

Parallax is for decoration only — never for text, cards or anything a reader has to catch.

Budget

One reveal per element, never nested. No more than three things animating in a viewport at once. Only transform and opacity animate — never width, height, top or margin. The whole runtime is a single 2 KB file, motion.js, and it degrades to plain, visible content when JavaScript or motion is switched off.
